# Episode 00 Mission: Start Your Path

Pick **one** level. Starter is a real win, and you don't have to do all three.

## Starter (≈ 10 min · no GitHub needed)

Post a comment under the Episode 00 video in this format:

```text
Start: what you can do today (e.g., "some JavaScript, no Python")
Goal: where you want to get to (e.g., "build and evaluate a RAG app")
Hours/week: an honest number
```

Keep it general. Don't post your email, phone number, employer or location.

**Done when:** your comment is posted.

## Core (≈ 20–30 min · free GitHub account)

1. Open [github.com/SoroushJaberi/the-ai-path](https://github.com/SoroushJaberi/the-ai-path).
2. Click **Fork** (top-right). That makes your own copy of the repo.
3. In your fork, open `README.md`, click the ✏️ edit button, and tick **Ep 00** on the progress checklist (change `- [ ]` to `- [x]`). Commit the change.
4. From Sat 7 Nov: open the Episode 01 folder and check that its notebook opens in Colab.

**Done when:** your fork shows Ep 00 ticked.

## Stretch (≈ 30–45 min)

In your fork, create a file called `LEARNING-CONTRACT.md` with these five lines:

```text
1. My goal, in one sentence:
2. My track: Data Science / AI Engineering / not sure yet
3. Hours per week I can really give:
4. My fixed study slot (day + time):
5. My first checkpoint (a date + what I'll have finished by then):
```

Optional: share the link in this repo's **Discussions** tab, under **Introductions**.

**Done when:** the file is in your fork.
